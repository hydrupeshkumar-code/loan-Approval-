## Description

Home-loan applications with applicant and co-applicant income, loan amount and term, credit history, education, employment, dependents, property area, and a binary loan decision. Loan_Status Y means approved and N means rejected. Gender is present for audit and is not used as a model input.

## Columns

Loan_ID, Gender, Married, Dependents, Education, Self_Employed, ApplicantIncome, CoapplicantIncome, LoanAmount, Loan_Amount_Term, Credit_History, Property_Area, Loan_Status
